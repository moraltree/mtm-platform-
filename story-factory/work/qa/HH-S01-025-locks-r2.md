### Sherlock: verdict PASS after one wording fix
- **S-r2-1 (R2-3). The jolt geometry at l.219 is confused.** The troughs run round the *outer* path, from the cottage door to the back wall. A jolt that travels "all the way round the path to the middle of the garden" does not make sense spatially. Bees react to vibration through what they stand on, so the mechanism is right, but the route is muddled. Suggested wording: "…the whole line of troughs jolted on its stones, and the thump went through the ground and across the beds to the middle of the garden, where the bee gums stood."
- **Verified:**
  - **Counterweight.** A stone heavier than a full bucket raises it; the empty bucket must then be pulled down (l.142).
  - **Rain butts.** A raised butt under a downpipe, with a tap and a notched lid, is correct (l.248, l.321). One night of steady rain refilling a butt from a cottage roof is plausible. The butt was emptied by the 42-can soak on Saturday, so "full to the brim" on Sunday morning is consistent.
  - **Mulching.** Water first and then mulch, three fingers deep, kept off the stems: correct (l.323–331). Mulch cuts evaporation and how often you need to water, and six cans a day after a deep soak is a reasonable reading of that.
  - **Runner beans and drought.** Runner beans flowering with first pods in early August; drought yellowing that does not reverse (l.372); new growth after rain. All correct.
  - **The troughs.** Dry wood absorbs water at first; joins leak; creek clay seals a crack and then cracks again (l.113, l.168, l.280). Correct.
  - **Water and slope.** Water will not run uphill without lifting (l.64, l.117–119). Correct.
  - **Bees.** "Dozens" from the one gum, nobody stung, everyone moving away quickly and quietly, the experienced adult calming things: correct practice.
  - **Dusk details.** Swallows skimming low at dusk and bats coming out are correct. But see Dr Noura N-r2-1 about the swallows "catching" insects.
  - **The sky.** The sun sets behind Weather Ridge, in the west.
  - **Arithmetic.** 84 × 30 = 2,520; 84 × 7 = 588; 84 − 12 = 72; 84 − 6 = 78. All correct.

## LOCK THREE: "Dr Noura" (safety and bedtime suitability) and narration readiness

### Dr Noura: verdict PASS after one wording fix
- **N-r2-1 (R2-4). Possible breach of a Bible §15 prohibition: "catching the last of the evening insects" (l.442).** §15 forbids "no animals eating one another, and no reference to predator-and-prey". In this world insects (the bees) are fauna, and swallows catching insects is predation, however gentle. No earlier S01 story has done this. It is a cheap fix: "the swallows were skimming low, back and forth, over the water." The bats coming out (l.450) are fine as written.
- **All round 1 safety conditions are met:**
  - Grown-ups lift the stone, and Brody only steadies the rope.
  - Only Brody's father touches the lever.
  - Everyone stands well back from both sides of the tower.
  - Both barrels have lids, explicitly "so that nothing and nobody could ever fall in".
  - The clay comes from the dry edge of the creek bank.
  - The bee scare is handled by walking away quietly, with nobody stung.
  - Brody's father hauls and sets the butt.
  - The children only work the low tap and the watering can.
- Barney "had just that moment stepped forward to look up" (l.156). The bucket is roped and the result is only a soaking, so this is acceptable as comedy. Advisory only: Brody's father's "Back you come" would model the rule being restored.
- **Bedtime suitability is excellent.** The close slows deliberately: counting slower, the boards still warm, the smell of wet grass, falling asleep at seventeen, the swallows uncounted. Benny asleep on Buck's porch at dusk, with everybody (adults included) present, raises no safety issue.
- **Tone.** No sarcasm, menace or dated references. Barney's teasing ("Behold") and Rusty's "Nobody wrote mine down" are warm. Charlie being corrected at l.424 is gentle and comes with an explanation, not a put-down.

### Narration readiness: READY after R2-1 and R2-2
