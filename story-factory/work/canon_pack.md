# Hickory Hollow canon pack for reviewers (compiled 17 Nov 2026)

Sources: Franchise Bible v1.1 (founder-approved character canon, incl. 17 Nov amendments) governs cast; Bible v1.0 (full text at ../bible/v1_0.txt) governs world, geography, festivals, Mountain Wisdom, style and prohibitions where not superseded. Later founder decisions override both.

## Locked cast — exactly eleven (Bible v1.1)
1. GREAT-GRANDPAPPY BUCK — male elderly beaver. Patient, wise, gentle, practical; whittling, banjo on his porch, storytelling. Visual: denim dungarees, sturdy country boots, cap and glasses, kind grandfatherly face; rustic cabin/porch, banjo, walking stick. Teaches through stories, questions, gentle guidance; must NOT simply solve every child's challenge. Late wife Great-Grandmammy Bramble (spoken of warmly, never grief). Hickory rocking chair; Bramble's patchwork quilt.
2. BENNY — male young beaver, Barney's brother. Thoughtful, practical, inventive; building, cooperation, practical problem-solving. (v1.0: careful planner, measures twice, carpenter's pencil behind ear, a touch too serious.)
3. BARNEY — male young beaver, Benny's brother. Playful, energetic, adventurous; own personality (v1.0: free spirit, turns chores into games, splashes, heedless, younger brother).
4. BRODY — male young black bear cub. Warm-hearted, friendly, gentle. Visual (Rev 2): dark soft brown-black fur, creamy tummy/chest, muzzle, lighter inner ears; red neckerchief; small green explorer's rucksack. NO sports jerseys/NFL branding. Founder default: no orphan backstory unless approved.
5. REBA — female raccoon. Curious, charming, gently mischievous, fond of food; investigates cupboards and pantries but learns to respect others' belongings; helps neighbours; never malicious or dishonest by nature. Visual: soft grey-brown fur, cream tummy/muzzle, mask, bright eyes, bushy ringed tail, pink bow, red/pink floral neckerchief; optional small green rucksack.
6. RUSTY — male young red fox pup. Lively, adventurous, resourceful. Preserve v1.0 Finn habits: shortcuts ("Trust me, I know a shortcut"), scout, whistles birdsong, white-tipped tail, amber eyes. Never a stereotypically deceitful fox.
7. SADIE BELLE — female young deer (NOT a skunk). Green eyes, pink floral headband, matching pink neckerchief. Gentle, caring, curious, encouraging. Innocent crush on Beau, largely unnoticed by him.
8. MAVERICK — male young American bald eagle (replaces retired Hattie the owl). Bold, adventurous, handsome, charismatic, self-assured, quarterback swagger; a little brash and comical, but loyal, kind, eager to prove himself; confidence never eclipses kindness. Visual: youthful white-feathered head, blue eyes, brown wings, yellow beak and talons, tan explorer vest with mountain patches, red neckerchief. NO aviator jacket.
9. BEAU — male young bobcat. Brilliant blue eyes, bright white markings, tawny spotted coat, black ear tufts, forest-green vest, golden-yellow neckerchief. Athletic, daring, loyal, warm-hearted, slightly goofy, occasionally clueless. Optional nickname "Beau Blue Eyes".
10. MAGGIE MAE — female young black-and-white striped skunk (replaces Wyatt). Emerald-green eyes, magnificent fluffy tail, pink polka-dot bow, yellow floral outfit. Sophisticated, elegant, organised, warm-hearted Southern belle, generous hostess; flowers, baking, gardening. Smells lovely, NEVER unpleasant. Best friend and affectionate big-sister mentor to Charlie.
11. CHARLOTTE ("CHARLIE") — female young Eastern chipmunk (formerly Pip). Warm-brown and cream striped fur, bright blue eyes, purple gingham bow, denim overalls with daisy motif. Young, enthusiastic, curious, creative, loving, inexperienced. "Charlotte" formally, "Charlie" in conversation. Maggie Mae is her mentor; bond is reciprocal.

RETIRED / MUST NOT APPEAR as active characters: Hattie, Hazel, Wyatt, Willow, Daisy, Pip, Boone, Rosie, Finn, Tucker. Benny & Barney: present in the Hollow; NO hollow-log departure or River Rangers connection established (v1.1; founder default for S01-030: a hint only).

## World (v1.0) — key places
Silver Creek; Whisper Falls (dry cave behind); Hickory Woods; Fern Meadow (festivals); seven old wooden bridges over Silver Creek, oldest Grandpappy's Bridge near Buck's lodge; Beaver Ponds; Blackberry Bank; Lookout Rock (Sunrise Ridge); Echo Cave; three ridges: Sunrise (east), Weather (west), High Ridge (behind, tallest); fishing holes Old Faithful, Sunny Bend, Grandpappy's Pool; Wildflower Glades; the Great Barn in Fern Meadow; the Old Mill; the Story Stones; the Sugar Grove; the Hollow Tree. Festivals: Blossom Gathering (spring), Midsummer Music Evening, Harvest Home (autumn), Longest Night (winter). Food: cornbread, berry pies, hickory-nut cake, maple, honey, hot spiced berry-cordial. Sayings: "That'll mend", "Warm as Buck's porch". Mountain Wisdom: 109 numbered sayings in v1.0 section 8.

## Absolute prohibitions (v1.0 §15) and tone
No hunting; no animals eating one another / predator-prey references; no cruelty, frightening villains or genuine menace; no violence; no unnecessary sadness; no cynicism, sarcasm, meanness, snark; nothing frightening at bedtime; no topical, technological, branded or dated references; no humour at a character's expense / put-downs; Buck's pipe never lit (founder default: avoid the pipe). Tone: warm, timeless, gentle, funny, comforting, hopeful. Lessons shown, not preached; Buck's wisdom the only "telling".

## Founder decisions applied for Season One (recorded 17 Nov 2026 under the instruction "Just continue everything approved", applying Merlin's recommended defaults)
- Porch-and-wisdom close in most stories, staging varied.
- Buck is the banjo master; Brody plays along / is learning.
- Brody: no orphan backstory.
- Reba: v1.1 curiosity/food first; light tinkering only.
- Safeguarding: adults handle blades, flames, roofs, ice, machinery; children not left in danger; supervised outings.

## Manuscript rules
Exactly ten chapters; 5,000–6,000 narrated words (already machine-counted: do not recount); British English; authentic gentle Southern Appalachian atmosphere without exaggerated dialect; no generic AI phrasing, padding, circular dialogue, formulaic moral speeches, recycled endings.
