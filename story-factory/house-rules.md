# Story Factory: house rules learned from Triple-Lock round 1 (S01 001–008)

Every Batch 1 draft, and 006 and 007 in Batch 2, failed Lock One on its first
review (scores 7.6–8.1) for the same reasons. Check each new draft against this
list before it goes to review.

1. **The moral is stated once.** Buck's Mountain Wisdom says it, close to word
   for word as written in v1.0, with no lead-in that summarises the lesson.
   Neither the narrator nor any character spells out the theme earlier in the
   story.
2. **The children solve the problem.** Buck and other adults may ask one
   question, keep things safe, or remember something only after the
   children have found it. They never supply the method or the key reasoning.
3. **The ensemble does real work.** The leads, plus at least three other
   friends, each make a contribution that moves the plot and fits their
   character. Nobody is just decoration.
4. **The comedy escalates on stage** in chapters 2–4. Guilt and sadness
   sections stay short. There is a real turning point.
5. **The close is fresh every time.** Vary where it happens. Do not reuse any
   of these: "first slow creak … the porch went quiet", the quilt over the
   smallest knees, the banjo taken from its corner, the first star over Sunrise
   Ridge, friends falling asleep one by one, Reba's crumb, a head tucked under a
   wing, "Nobody said anything", or a closing image of an object glowing in the
   moonlight.
6. **Banned stock phrases:** lit up like a lamp, biggest grin, a breath they'd
   been holding, something big and warm in the chest, long silence, eyes shone,
   close but not too close, bow over one ear. Use "the way X does" similes no
   more than once per story.
7. **Safety:** adults handle flames, blades, roofs, ice, machinery and water
   tubs. Outings are supervised. Nobody walks alone in the dark. Nobody pushes
   anyone into water. Never look straight at the sun.
8. **Facts:** check all natural history and physics before drafting.
9. **Continuity:** say the day in the text whenever time passes. Keep counts
   consistent. Keep seasonal order: S01 runs autumn 001–008, winter 009–015,
   spring 016–022, summer 023–030.
10. **Keep new canon to a minimum.** Prefer places that already exist in v1.0.
    List every new detail for the founder.
11. **Lie plots:** at most one untruth, and the story shows telling a grown-up.
12. **Length:** draft about 5,600 words, because first drafts come in about 10%
    shorter than intended. The hard limits are 5,000–6,000 narrated words.
