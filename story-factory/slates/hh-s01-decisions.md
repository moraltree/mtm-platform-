# HH Season One — founder decision log

## 17 Nov 2026 — production authorised

**"Just continue everything approved."**

Merlin read this as two things. First, it authorises Season One manuscript
production. Second, it approves the defaults Merlin recommended for every open
decision. Those defaults are listed below. Any of them can be changed by the
founder at any time. Stories already drafted will then be revised.
